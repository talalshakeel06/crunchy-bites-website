Our Story

A home kitchen that grew into 12,000 followers of word-of-mouth.

Crunchy Bites started the way most good bakeries do — with one recipe, tested until it was right, shared with a few neighbours.

How We Bake

Small batches, real ingredients, nothing rushed.

It's still run the same way today: small batches, real butter and chocolate, and orders that are baked only after you confirm them.

Every box is packed by hand in Karachi, whether it's a single brownie tray for a birth announcement or a standing order of desserts for a corporate event.
